#  project brief

## Part 1:The question.
Which area in Ona ara local government area, oyo sate are more than 5km from a health facility?

## Part 2: Why It Matters.
Public health planners at the Oyo State Primary Health Care Board and local NGO emergency responders 
can use this analysis to pinpoint under served communities in Ona Ara. Instead of distributing 
health resources uniformly, decision makers can deploy mobile clinic routes or prioritize the construction
of new Primary Health Centres (PHCs) specifically within these high-deficit zones.

## Part 3: The Data You Need.
Nigeria Operational Health Facilities point dataset for Oyo State.

Nigeria Operational Ward Boundaries polygon dataset for Oyo State (including Ona Ara LGA).

OpenStreetMap (OSM) highway/road network lines for Ibadan and Ona Ara LGA.

## Part 4: Where Each Dataset Comes From.
GRNigeria Health Facilities v3.0: GRID3 Data Hub https://data.grid3.org

GRID3 Nigeria Operational Wards v3.0: GRID3 Data Hub https://data.grid3.org

OpenStreetMap Road Network: the QuickOSM plugin in QGIS.

## Part 5: What You Would Build
An interactive map visualizing health facilities accessibility.
